# Code of Conduct

We want Covo's community to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful. Report problems privately to the maintainers (repository → Security → Report, or the contact listed on the maintainer's profile).

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
